How to convert PDF to JPG on Mac (every page at once)

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later

To turn a page or two of a PDF into JPG on a Mac, select the page in Preview, choose File → Export, and save as JPEG. Export saves one page at a time, so converting every page means repeating it for each page. To convert all pages at once, an app is quicker. Neither method uploads your files.

A portfolio needs to go up as one image per page, or you need to send a single page of a contract as a picture. Online converters cap the page count or make you upload the file.

1. Preview (a page or two)

  1. Open the PDF in Preview and choose View → Thumbnails.
  2. Select the page you want.
  3. Choose File → Export, set Format to JPEG, and save.

Export saves only the page you are on. For 10 pages, you repeat it 10 times.

2. Every page at once: Dropfit for Mac

Dropfit for Mac is an app I built.

  1. Drag the PDF onto the circle on your desktop.
  2. Drop it on JPG among the formats that fan out. Before you let go, it shows how many images you'll get, for example "5 pages → 5 JPG images."
  3. A folder named "File name (JPG)" appears next to the PDF, with the pages saved in order as numbered images.
  • A one-page PDF is saved as "File name.jpg", without a folder.
  • Pick PNG to get PNG images.
  • You can drop several PDFs or a whole folder.

Conversion is free, with no account and no limits.

FAQ

Can I convert a PDF to JPG on a Mac for free?

Yes. Export in Preview is built into macOS and free, and conversion in Dropfit for Mac is free too.

Can I convert every page of a PDF to JPG at once?

Preview's Export saves one page at a time. Drop the PDF on JPG in Dropfit and every page is saved, in order, to a "File name (JPG)" folder.

Can I get PNG instead of JPG?

Yes. In Preview, choose PNG as the format. In Dropfit, drop the PDF on PNG.
