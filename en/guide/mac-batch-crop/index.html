How to crop many photos to the same ratio on Mac (1:1, 4:5)

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later

Preview, the Mac's built-in viewer, crops one photo at a time from a selection you draw. To crop many photos to the same ratio, an app that puts the same frame on every photo is quicker. In Dropfit for Mac you drop the photos, pick 1:1, 4:5, 3:4 or 16:9, move the frame only where needed, and save them all at once.

Ten photos for an Instagram post at 4:5, or product shots that all need to be square: cropping them one by one in Preview, the ratios drift a little each time. Online croppers work, but your originals go to someone else's server.

1. Preview, one at a time (free)

  1. Open the photo in Preview.
  2. Choose Tools → Rectangular Selection and draw the area. Hold Shift while dragging for a square.
  3. Choose Tools → Crop, then save.

Ratios other than square, such as 4:5 or 3:4, you match by eye from the selection size, for every photo. Preview saves over the original, so duplicate it first if you need it.

2. Pick the ratio once: Dropfit for Mac

Cropping a set of photos to one ratio, one photo at a time, got tiresome, so I built Dropfit for Mac.

  1. Drag the photos onto the circle on your desktop and drop them on "Crop N images." You can also Control-click the circle and choose "Choose Images to Crop…"
  2. In the Crop window, pick a ratio: Free, 1:1, 4:5, 3:4, 16:9 or Custom.
  3. Every photo gets a frame at that ratio. Flip through and move the frame only on the photos that need it.
  4. Click Save. Files like "photo (cropped).jpg" are saved next to the originals, which stay untouched.

The same window sets the format (JPG, PNG and more) and a maximum file size, so cropping and shrinking happen in one step. For exact pixels, such as 1080×1350, enter the width and height in Custom.

Common ratios

  • 1:1: square Instagram posts, product photos
  • 4:5: portrait Instagram posts
  • 3:4: ID and portrait photos
  • 16:9: YouTube thumbnails, slides

FAQ

Can I crop many photos to the same ratio at once on a Mac?

Preview crops one photo at a time. Dropfit puts the same frame on every photo after you pick the ratio once, and saves them all together.

What if each photo needs a different crop position?

Flip through the photos in Dropfit's Crop window and move the frame. The ratio stays the same.

Are my originals replaced?

No. Dropfit saves new files such as "photo (cropped).jpg" next to the originals. Preview saves over the original, so duplicate it first.
