How to convert images on Mac: HEIC and PNG to JPG in bulk

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later

To convert many photos at once on a Mac, select them in Finder, Control-click, and choose Quick Actions → Convert Image. You can pick JPEG, PNG or HEIF. To set the quality of a single image, use Export in Preview. To convert a whole folder, or turn every page of a PDF into images, an app is quicker. None of these upload your files.

Dozens of iPhone photos need to be JPGs, and opening each one to export it gets old fast. Your Mac has two built-in ways to convert images, and each has limits.

1. Finder Quick Actions: Convert Image (many at once)

  1. Select the photos in Finder.
  2. Control-click and choose Quick Actions → Convert Image.
  3. Pick a format (JPEG, PNG or HEIF) and a size, then click Convert. New files appear in the same folder.

Only JPEG, PNG and HEIF are offered. Selecting a folder does not convert the photos inside it, so open the folder and select the photos themselves.

2. Preview: Export (one image, fine control)

  1. Open the image in Preview and choose File → Export.
  2. Pick a format. For JPEG, use the Quality slider.
  3. Save it under a new name.

You can judge quality image by image, but turning a multi-page PDF into images means exporting each page separately.

3. One drag and drop: Dropfit for Mac

Dropfit for Mac is an app I built. A small circle sits on your desktop; drag files onto it and the formats fan out.

  1. Drag photos, PDFs or a folder onto the circle.
  2. Drop them on one of the formats: JPG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF or PDF.
  3. New files are saved next to the originals. The originals are untouched.
  • Drop a whole folder to convert every photo inside it.
  • Drop a PDF on JPG to get one image per page.

Format conversion, folder conversion and merging PDFs are free, with no account and no limits.

FAQ

Can I convert HEIC to JPG on a Mac for free?

Yes. Finder's Convert Image Quick Action and Preview's Export are built into macOS and free. Format conversion in Dropfit for Mac is free too.

What happens to the original photos?

All three methods create new files and leave the originals as they are. Dropfit saves the new files next to the originals.

How do I convert a WebP image saved from the web to JPG?

Open it in Preview and choose File → Export, then set Format to JPEG. For many files, drop them on JPG in Dropfit and they are converted at once.

Can I convert every photo in a folder at once?

Finder's Convert Image does not convert photos inside a selected folder, so you select the photos themselves. In Dropfit, drop the whole folder and every photo inside is converted.
