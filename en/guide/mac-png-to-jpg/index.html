How to convert PNG to JPG on Mac (many at once)

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later

The fastest built-in way to convert PNG to JPG on a Mac is to select the PNG files in Finder, Control-click, choose Quick Actions → Convert Image, and set Format to JPEG. For a single file, Preview's Export works too. To convert a whole folder, an app is quicker. JPG can't store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with a solid color.

Screenshots and design files are saved as PNG, but plenty of forms and shops only accept JPG. PNGs are also larger, so converting them to JPG often gets you under an upload limit.

1. Finder Quick Actions: Convert Image (many at once)

  1. Select the PNG files in Finder.
  2. Control-click and choose Quick Actions → Convert Image.
  3. Set Format to JPEG and click Convert. New files appear in the same folder.

Selecting a folder does not convert the files inside it, so open the folder and select the files themselves.

2. Preview: Export (one at a time)

  1. Open the PNG file in Preview and choose File → Export.
  2. Set Format to JPEG and choose the quality.
  3. Save it under a new name.

You can judge the quality of each image, but for many files you repeat the steps for each one.

3. One drag and drop: Dropfit for Mac

Dropfit for Mac is an app I built. A small circle sits on your desktop; drag files onto it and the formats fan out.

  1. Drag PNG files, or a folder of them, onto the circle.
  2. Drop them on JPG.
  3. New files are saved next to the originals. The originals are untouched.

Drop a whole folder to convert every file inside it. Format conversion is free, with no account and no limits.

FAQ

Can I convert PNG to JPG on a Mac for free?

Yes. Finder's Convert Image Quick Action and Preview's Export are built into macOS and free. Format conversion in Dropfit for Mac is free too.

What happens to a transparent PNG background in JPG?

JPG can't store transparency. Dropfit fills transparent areas with white so they don't turn black. If you need the transparency, keep the PNG.

Does converting PNG to JPG make the file smaller?

For photos and screenshots with lots of color, usually by a lot. For images that are mostly text and flat color, the difference can be small.
