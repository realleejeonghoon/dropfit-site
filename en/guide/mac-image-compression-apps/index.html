Best image compression apps for Mac, by situation

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later · Written by the maker of Dropfit

For one or two photos, the built-in Preview app is enough. For lossless compression of web images, use ImageOptim. To tune a single image while comparing before and after, Squoosh in the browser works well. If you often need many photos or a whole folder under a size like 1 MB, an app that fits files to a size you set (Clop, Tangerine or Dropfit) is fastest.

When Dropfit isn't the right fit

  • If you only shrink a photo now and then, Preview is enough.
  • If you just want lighter website images at the same quality, ImageOptim fits better.
  • If you also need to compress video or audio, use Tangerine or a video app. Dropfit handles images and PDFs only.

FAQ

What is the best free way to compress photos on a Mac?

For one or two photos, open them in Preview, choose File → Export, pick JPEG and lower the Quality slider. For many photos, Finder's Quick Actions → Convert Image makes them smaller in one step. For lossless compression of web images, ImageOptim is free and open source.

Which Mac app keeps every photo under a set size like 1 MB?

Preview, Finder and ImageOptim don't fit files to a size you set. Clop (Fit under size), Tangerine and Dropfit Pro presets do. In Dropfit for Mac you set a maximum JPG size on a preset once, then drop photos or a whole folder on it; each one is saved under that size next to the original.

Is it safe to upload ID or document photos to an online compressor?

It depends on the site. Squoosh says images never leave your device. If you can't tell, use an app that works on your Mac, such as Preview, ImageOptim or Dropfit.

Do these apps work on Intel Macs?

Preview, Finder, ImageOptim and Dropfit work on Intel Macs. Dropfit runs on macOS 14 or later on both Apple Silicon and Intel. Tangerine requires Apple Silicon (M1 or later).
