Email attachment over 25 MB? Send design PDFs to clients as attachments

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later

Gmail lets you send up to 25 MB of attachments in total. Go over, and it puts a Google Drive link in the email instead. If the files have to arrive as attachments, shrink the PDFs and images on your Mac until the total is under 25 MB. Preview’s Quartz filter does it for free; if you need a set size for each file, an app that saves to a maximum size is faster.

It’s Friday evening. You drag the rebrand PDF and six mockup images into Gmail, and Gmail puts in a Google Drive link instead of attaching them. The client writes back: their company blocks outside Drive links, so could you attach the files instead? Re-exporting means opening the design app and redoing the settings file by file.

Designers who deliver concepts and proposals by email, and marketers who send press kits with images, run into this all the time. A compression site is the obvious search result, but an unreleased concept on a server you don’t know is its own worry. On a Mac you can shrink the files without uploading them.

First: 25 MB is the total of all attachments

  • Gmail’s 25 MB covers every attachment in one email, not each file. A 15 MB PDF plus six 2 MB images is 27 MB, which is over.
  • Select the files in Finder and press Control-Command-I. The Summary Info window shows the total size.
  • Leave some room under 25 MB rather than aiming right at it, so Gmail doesn’t switch to a link at the last step.

1. Shrink the PDF (Preview Quartz filter, free)

  1. Open the PDF in Preview.
  2. Choose File → Export and set Quartz Filter to Reduce File Size.
  3. Save it under a new name, then open it and check the size and the images.

You can’t pick a target size, and photos in the PDF can come out too blurry to show a client. An already compressed PDF can even get bigger, so always check the result.

2. Shrink the mockups (Finder Quick Actions, free)

  1. Select the images in Finder.
  2. Control-click and choose Quick Actions → Convert Image.
  3. Set Format to JPEG and Image Size to Medium or Large, then convert.

New files appear next to the originals. You choose a size, not a file size, so you only see how many MB you got after converting.

3. Set a size for each file once: Dropfit for Mac

I built Dropfit for Mac. Instead of choosing settings every time you send, you set an email size once and just drag files onto it.

  1. Control-click the circle on your desktop, open Settings, and set a maximum size on a PDF preset (for example, 15 MB) and on a JPG preset (for example, 1 MB). You only do this once.
  2. Drop the design PDF on the PDF preset and the mockups on the JPG preset.
  3. Files under the size you set are saved next to the originals. The originals stay as they are.

In a PDF with selectable text, the text stays as it is and only the images inside are recompressed. If a file can’t reach the size you set, Dropfit keeps the smallest version and tells you its size. Nothing is uploaded.

To send several images as one PDF, drop them together on PDF on the circle. Merging is free.

If the file shouldn’t be shrunk

Print-ready originals shouldn’t lose quality, so send them as a link instead. In the Mail app on your Mac, Mail Drop sends up to 5 GB as an iCloud link that stays available for 30 days. Attach a small review PDF and add the original as a link, and the client gets both.

FAQ

Is Gmail’s 25 MB limit per file?

No. It’s the total of all attachments in one email. With several files, they have to add up to less than 25 MB to go as attachments.

How do I stop Gmail from turning attachments into a Drive link?

Get the total under 25 MB. Shrink PDFs with Preview’s Quartz filter or a Dropfit PDF preset, and convert images to JPEG at a smaller size.

How do I send a big file from the Mail app on Mac?

Turn on Mail Drop, and large attachments go as an iCloud link. It handles up to 5 GB, and the link stays available for 30 days.

Does Dropfit send my files to a server?

No. Shrinking happens entirely on your Mac, and files and file names are never sent anywhere.
