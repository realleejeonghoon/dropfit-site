How to merge PDFs without uploading them

November 2026 · Mac (macOS 14 or later) and Windows

Online PDF merge sites are convenient, but to merge your files they have to upload them to their servers. On a Mac you don’t need to install anything: select the files in Finder, then Control-click → Quick Actions → Create PDF, and a single combined PDF appears right there. On Windows, use a PDF app that works without an internet connection, and your files never leave your PC.

When a form says “upload one PDF,” most people search “merge PDF” and open the first site. But the files are often things you’d rather keep private: ID scans, tax forms, bank statements, signed contracts, medical records.

What happens on a PDF merge site

  1. Your PDFs travel over the internet to the site’s server.
  2. The server combines them into a new PDF.
  3. You download the result. The originals and the result may stay on the server for a while.

How long files are kept, and how they’re deleted, varies by site. If you must use one, check the privacy policy for the file retention period first. Free sites often limit how many files or how large a file you can merge, and large files take time to upload and download.

Mac: merge right away, nothing to install

  1. In Finder, select all the PDFs and photos you want to combine.
  2. Control-click (or right-click) → Quick Actions → Create PDF.
  3. A combined PDF appears in the same folder. Your originals stay untouched.

There’s no screen to set the order, so if order matters, add numbers like 01, 02 to the front of the file names. To reorder individual pages in Preview, see How to combine PDFs on Mac.

Mac: if you merge often, Dropfit

Dropfit for Mac is an app I built. Drop files onto “PDF” on the small circle on your desktop, set the order and file name in one window, and they become one PDF. The result is saved next to the first file, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Merging PDFs is free, with no account and no limits.

Windows: pick an app that works offline

On Windows, a PDF app installed on your PC can merge files without uploading them. Some installed apps still do the merging on a server, so the first time, turn off Wi-Fi and try a merge. If it works offline, the app does the work on your PC.

Before you merge

  • Check the upload size limit first. Scans and photos make combined PDFs large quickly. See reducing PDF size on Mac.
  • Remove the password from protected PDFs before merging.
  • If the form asks for a specific file name, rename the combined file.

FAQ

Are online PDF merge sites safe?

They receive your files on their servers, combine them and send the result back. How long files are kept varies by site, so read the privacy policy first, and combine sensitive documents like ID scans, tax forms and contracts on your own computer.

Can I merge PDFs for free on a Mac?

Yes. Finder’s Create PDF Quick Action and Preview are built into macOS, and merging PDFs in Dropfit for Mac is free too. None of them upload your files.

How can I tell if a PDF app uploads my files?

Turn off Wi-Fi and try merging. If it still works, the app does the work on your computer.
