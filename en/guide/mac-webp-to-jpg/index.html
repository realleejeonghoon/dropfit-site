How to convert WebP to JPG on Mac

November 2026 · macOS 14 or later

If an image you saved from a web page ends in .webp, open it in Preview on your Mac, choose File → Export, set Format to JPEG, and save. To convert many files or a whole folder, an app is quicker. Neither method uploads your files.

You save an image from a website, it ends in .webp, and your editor or an upload form won't take it. WebP is made to keep web pages light; for anything else, JPG or PNG is the safer choice.

1. Preview: Export (one at a time)

  1. Open the WebP file in Preview.
  2. Choose File → Export, set Format to JPEG, and choose the quality.
  3. Save it under a new name.

For many files, you repeat this for each one.

2. One drag and drop: Dropfit for Mac

Dropfit for Mac is an app I built. A small circle sits on your desktop; drag files onto it and the formats fan out.

  1. Drag WebP files, or a folder of them, onto the circle.
  2. Drop them on JPG or PNG.
  3. New files are saved next to the originals. The originals are untouched.

Drop a whole folder to convert every file inside it. Format conversion is free, with no account and no limits.

FAQ

What is a WebP file?

An image format used to keep web pages light. Images saved from websites often end in .webp, and some apps and upload forms don't accept it.

Can I convert WebP to JPG on a Mac for free?

Yes. Preview's Export is built into macOS and free, and format conversion in Dropfit for Mac is free too.

Can I convert many WebP files at once?

Drop several files or a whole folder on JPG in Dropfit and they are all converted at once.
