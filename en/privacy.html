5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our own website code sets no cookies. To count visits, it keeps a note of how you arrived in your browser tab storage, which is cleared when you close the tab, and it remembers in your browser whether you already signed up so we do not ask again. Neither identifies you. The pricing section and checkout are provided by Paddle, which may use cookies for payment and fraud prevention. You can clear or block site data in your browser settings, and downloading Dropfit still works.

The emails we send contain a tiny image and tracked links that record when an email is opened and when a link is followed. To avoid this, turn off automatic image loading in your email app.

6. Deletion

When the retention period ends, we permanently delete the record from Google Sheets and the email from Gmail.

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your data, object to its use, or receive a copy. Email leejeonghoon.official@gmail.com and we will respond within 10 days. 8. Security

Only Paddle receives card and other payment details; Dropfit does not. The Google, GitHub and Cloudflare accounts that handle personal data use two-step verification, and only one person has access. The website, sign-up and update checks are all encrypted (HTTPS), and your license key is stored only in your Mac's keychain.
